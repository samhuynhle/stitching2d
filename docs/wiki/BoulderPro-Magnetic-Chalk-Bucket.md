# 🧗 Project Spotlight: BoulderPro Magnetic Chalk Bucket (v8.0.0)

The **BoulderPro** is an ultra-durable, 100% zipper-free trapezoidal bouldering chalk bucket with dual magnetic openings, monolithic continuous U-panel cradle, and aerodynamic dust baffles.

---

* **Footprint:** $10.5''\text{ Wide} \times 8.0''\text{ Deep}$ ($84\text{ sq in}$ anti-tip wide stance).
* **Mouth Opening:** $8.5''\text{ Wide} \times 6.0''\text{ Deep}$ (fits 2 hands dipped simultaneously).
* **Height:** $12.0''\text{ Total}$ ($11.0''$ upper taper $+ 1.0''$ bottom stash slot).
* **Internal Volume:** $\approx 4.2\text{ Liters}$ (unified deep fleece interior).

---

## 🧲 100% Zipper-Free Magnetic Closure System

* **Mouth Seal (8x $18\text{mm} \times 2\text{mm}$ N52 Magnets):** 4 pairs encased inside Ultrasuede microfiber.
* **Bottom Access Flap (6x $15\text{mm} \times 1.5\text{mm}$ N52 Magnets):** 3 pairs along the front $1''$ upward-folding lip.
* **Front 3D Gear Pocket (4x $15\text{mm} \times 1.5\text{mm}$ N52 Magnets):** 2 pairs for the X-Pac storm flap.
* **Rear Stash Pocket (2x $15\text{mm} \times 1.5\text{mm}$ N52 Magnets):** 1 pair locking the center mesh sleeve.
* **Total Magnets:** 20 Neodymium N52 discs (10 matched pairs). 0 zippers to jam with chalk powder!
